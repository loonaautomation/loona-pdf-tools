Frequently Asked Questions

Is the downloaded signature really transparent?

Yes. The PNG carries a true alpha channel: everywhere except the ink is fully or partly transparent, so it blends over form lines and text like real pen on paper - no white box.

Which method looks most like my real signature?

Drawing with a stylus or finger, or uploading a clear photo of your real signature on white paper. Typed styles are neat and consistent, but they use a device font rather than your own hand.

Why does the typed style look different on another device?

The typed styles use handwriting fonts installed on the device you are using - no font files are downloaded. Each system has slightly different script fonts, and the preview always shows exactly what will download.

Is my signature uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. Drawing, typing, background removal and the PNG download all happen in your browser. Your signature never leaves your device.

How it works

This tool builds your signature on an HTML canvas, entirely in your browser. In Draw mode your strokes are stored as individual paths, so Undo can remove the last stroke and changing pen colour or width only affects new strokes. In Type mode your name is rendered in a handwriting-style font installed on your device - no external or paid font assets are loaded. In Upload mode the photo is converted pixel by pixel from a white background to transparency. Before download, the tool can scan the alpha channel, find the bounding box of the visible ink, and crop away empty edges. The result is a standard transparent PNG you can reuse anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

How does the white-background removal work?

For every pixel the tool measures how far its darkest channel is from white. Pixels at or near white become transparent, dark ink stays solid, and grey edge pixels become partly transparent so strokes keep smooth edges. The strength slider controls how aggressively light-grey paper shading is removed.

How do I get the cleanest result from a photo?

Sign in dark blue or black ink on plain white paper, photograph it straight-on in bright, even light with no shadows, and crop close to the signature. Then raise the removal strength until the paper tint disappears without eating into the ink.

What size is the downloaded PNG?

Drawn and uploaded signatures keep their working resolution (640 px wide pad, or your photo's own size); typed signatures are rendered large and then trimmed. With Trim ticked, empty transparent edges are cropped away with a small safety margin.

Can I sign a PDF directly from this page?

This page makes the signature image. To place it on a PDF, open our Sign PDF tool and upload this PNG as the signature image - it positions and sizes it on any page with a live preview.

Is a signature image legally binding?

A pasted signature image is a simple electronic signature. It is widely accepted for everyday forms, but rules differ by country and document type; high-value legal contracts may require a certified e-signature service.
