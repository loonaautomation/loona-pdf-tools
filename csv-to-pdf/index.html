CSV to PDF Converter

In short: this tool turns CSV data into a tidy PDF table — header row repeated on every page, built in your browser.

Spreadsheets exported as CSV are data, not documents. This converter lays them out as a real table in a PDF you can attach, print or archive.

Private by design: your files are processed 100% in your browser with JavaScript. They are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How it works, step by step

  1. Paste CSV data or load a .csv file.
  2. Press Convert — the table is paginated automatically.
  3. Download the PDF and check the row/column report.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the first row become the header?

Yes — the first row is drawn bold on a shaded band and repeated at the top of every page, like a proper table.

What happens to very wide tables?

Column widths are fitted to the page (landscape by default); extremely long cell text is truncated with an ellipsis rather than breaking the layout — the status line reports the row and column count so you can verify.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The CSV is parsed and the PDF is built entirely in your browser.

Under the hood

The CSV parser handles quoted fields, embedded commas and doubled quotes. pdf-lib then draws the table with fitted column widths and a repeating header. It is a faithful table export — not a spreadsheet clone: formulas are exported as their stored values/text, and styling from Excel does not carry over, which the FAQ states plainly.

Worked example

A 240-row class result sheet pasted from Excel becomes a 6-page PDF with the header on every page — ready to print for the staff-room notice board.
