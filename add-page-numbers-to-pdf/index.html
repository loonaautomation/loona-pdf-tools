Add Page Numbers to PDF Online - Free

In short: the Add Page Numbers tool stamps a page number on every page of your PDF, in the style, position and size you choose, with an optional starting number.

Assignments, reports and legal bundles are easier to follow - and to reference in a meeting - with page numbers. Stamp them here in seconds: pick "Page 1", "1 of 10" or a plain number, place it top or bottom, and download.

How to add page numbers to a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF you want to number.
  2. Pick the number style, position, font size and the number to start from.
  3. Click "Add Page Numbers" and download the stamped PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I start numbering from a number other than 1?

Yes. Set the starting number - useful when this file continues another document.

Which positions are available?

Top or bottom, on the left, centre or right - six positions in total.

Will numbers cover my content?

Numbers are drawn in the margin area of each page. If your content runs to the very edge, choose the opposite position.

Are files uploaded for numbering?

No. Stamping happens entirely in your browser.
