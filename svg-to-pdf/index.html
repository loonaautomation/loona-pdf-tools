SVG to PDF Converter

In short: this tool converts SVG images into a clean PDF — one image per page, entirely in your browser.

Logos, diagrams and illustrations made as SVG need a fixed-layout twin for print shops, clients and portals that can't handle vector files.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert SVG to PDF?

Choose one or more SVG files above, pick a page size, and press Convert. Each image becomes a page of one PDF, in the order you selected them.

Will the quality change?

The SVG is rasterised crisply at up to 2048 px on its long side before being placed in the PDF — sharp for print at normal sizes. For infinite-scale vector output you would need a vector-native converter; this tool is honest about producing a high-resolution raster page.

Can I reorder the pages?

Files are added in selection order. For full control of order and layout, convert here, then use the Organize PDF tool to rearrange pages.

Is anything uploaded?

No — decoding and PDF building happen in your browser with pdf-lib.

Under the hood

The browser decodes each SVG image natively, draws it to a canvas, and pdf-lib embeds it as a page — fit-to-image pages use the image's own proportions (at 96 DPI), or the image is centred on A4/Letter with margins preserved.

Worked example

Three SVG screenshots of a chat support conversation, selected together, become a tidy 3-page PDF you can attach to a ticket — something no portal rejects, unlike the original format.
