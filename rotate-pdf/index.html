Rotate PDF Pages Online - Free

In short: the Rotate PDF tool permanently turns pages by 90, 180 or 270 degrees - either every page in the file or only a range you choose - and saves a corrected copy.

Scanned documents love to come out sideways. Instead of tilting your head (or your phone) forever, rotate the pages once and save a fixed PDF that opens the right way up in every reader.

Private by design: your files are processed 100% in your browser with JavaScript. They are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to rotate PDF pages

  1. Choose the PDF with the wrongly-turned pages.
  2. Pick the angle - 90, 180 or 270 degrees - and optionally limit it to a page range like 2-4.
  3. Click "Rotate PDF" and download the corrected document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes. The page rotation is written into the saved file, so every reader and printer shows the pages the new way.

Can I rotate just some pages?

Yes. Enter a range such as 2-4 (or single pages like 1, 3) in the pages box; leave it empty to rotate all pages.

Does rotation change quality?

No. Only the orientation flag of each page changes - content is untouched.

My pages are mixed - some right, some wrong. What then?

Rotate in two passes: first fix the majority, then run the file again rotating only the remaining pages.
