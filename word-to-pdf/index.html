Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with .doc files?

No — only modern .docx files work. The old binary .doc format cannot be read in a browser. If you have a .doc file, open it in Word or Google Docs and save or export it as .docx first, then convert it here.

Will the PDF look exactly like my Word document?

Not exactly — and we say so plainly. This converter is content-faithful, not layout-faithful: your text, headings, lists, tables and images come through clean and readable, but the exact page layout, columns, text boxes, SmartArt, shapes and precise fonts and spacing may change or be lost. For a pixel-perfect copy, print to PDF from Word itself.

Are headings, tables and images preserved?

Yes. Heading levels, bold and italic, bulleted and numbered lists, tables and inline images are all carried into the PDF in the right order. Two routes are offered: an instant on-device download, and a print view that uses your browser's own PDF engine for the closest layout.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

Never. The .docx file is opened and converted entirely in your browser with JavaScript libraries loaded from this site. Nothing is sent, stored or seen by us — close the tab and everything is gone.

Is it really free and unlimited?

Yes — free, unlimited, no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit imposed by us.

Under the hood

The .docx file is unpacked and turned into styled HTML by Mammoth.js (v1.13.0), then laid out into a real text-based PDF with pdf-lib. Both libraries are vendored in this site's own library folder — nothing is fetched from a CDN while you work, and nothing leaves your device. The print view writes the same styled HTML into a print-optimised page and lets your browser's own PDF engine render it, which is why its layout sits closest to the original. Neither route screenshots your document into images: the words stay as text you can select, copy and search.

Worked example

A teacher's two-page class notice — a heading, a few paragraphs, a bulleted list of dates and a small table — becomes a clean one-click PDF ready to share on WhatsApp or print for the notice board, without the file ever touching a server.
