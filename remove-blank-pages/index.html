Remove Blank Pages from PDF Online - Free

In short: the Remove Blank Pages tool scans every page of a PDF by actually rendering it and measuring how much ink it carries: pages below the coverage threshold you choose are listed with their exact percentages, then removed - so separator blanks from double-sided scans disappear without touching real content.

Scanning a double-sided stack in a simple feeder leaves a blank page after every sheet. Deleting them one by one is tedious; this tool finds them by looking at the pages the way a human would - by their pixels.

Threshold honesty: "blank" is a judgement call. A page holding only a page number or a faint stamp carries very little ink and may count as blank at a loose threshold. Always review the per-page coverage list before downloading - the tool shows every page percentage so nothing is removed silently.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool decide a page is blank?

Each page is rendered and its pixels are counted: any pixel noticeably darker than the paper background counts as ink. If ink covers less than your threshold percentage of the page, it is flagged blank. The exact percentage for every page is shown, so the decision is transparent.

Will a page with only a page number be removed?

Possibly, at the loose 1% threshold - in our own test a lone digit as a page number measured about 0.006% coverage, so it is flagged at the 0.05% and 0.2% settings and kept only at the ultra-strict 0.005% setting. Check the list before downloading: every page shows its measured percentage, so you can judge borderline pages yourself.

What about faint or light-grey content?

Pixels must be clearly darker than near-white to count as ink, so very faint pencil or a light watermark may not register. If a scan is faint overall, prefer the strict threshold and review the percentages.

Are removed pages recoverable?

Not from the downloaded file - keep your original. The cleaned copy contains only the pages that passed.

How it works

For each page, PDF.js renders a modest-resolution image in your browser. The tool samples its pixels and counts those whose brightness falls clearly below the paper-white background - text, lines, stamps and photos all register, blank paper does not. Dividing by the total pixels gives the ink coverage percentage shown in the list. On download, pdf-lib copies every page at or above your threshold into a fresh document, in the original order.

Worked example

A 40-page double-sided scan alternates content and blank backs. Analysis flags pages 2, 4, 6... at 0.00% while content pages show around 3%. One download later the file is a clean 20 pages.

Frequently asked questions

Why render pages instead of checking for text?

Many blank-page problems live in scanned PDFs, which have no text layer at all - a scan of an empty sheet and a scan of a full page look identical to a text check. Pixel analysis works for scans and digital documents alike.

Does analysis change my file?

No. Analysis only renders and measures. A new file is created only when you click the remove button, by copying the surviving pages with pdf-lib - their content is untouched.

What if every page is flagged blank?

The tool refuses to produce an empty PDF and tells you instead - that almost always means the threshold is too loose for that document.
