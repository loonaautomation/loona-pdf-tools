Free Certificate Maker Online - Printable Certificates

In short: type the recipient's name and a few details, watch the certificate design itself on the live A4 preview, then download a print-ready PDF (or PNG) - free, with no sign-up and no watermark. A whole class or course to certify? Switch to Bulk mode, paste one name per line, and get one PDF with a certificate for every name.

Certificates usually mean a design tool you cannot quite work, a template locked behind a paywall, or an awkward download fee at the last step. Here everything happens on this page: three ready-made designs, every word editable, a gold seal drawn in automatically - and the finished PDF is built in your browser, so names and details never leave your device.

Every other field above - course, date, issuer, signature and design - stays the same on every certificate; only the name changes. Blank lines are ignored and repeated names are kept.

Private by design: your certificate is designed and your PDF is built 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Names and details are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone. That matters with certificates: a class list of names stays on your own device.

How to make a certificate

  1. Pick one of the three designs and type your details - the title, recipient name, course line, description, date, issuer and signature name. The A4 landscape preview redraws itself as you type.
  2. For a single certificate, click "Download PDF" for a print-ready file, or "Download PNG" if you want an image to share.
  3. For many certificates at once, switch to Bulk mode, paste one name per line (up to 200), and click "Generate Bulk PDF" - you get one PDF with a page per name, ready to print.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this certificate maker really free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up and no watermark - including bulk mode. Many certificate sites let you design for free and then charge for the download; here the download is free too.

Are the names I type uploaded anywhere?

No. The certificate is drawn and the PDF is built 100% inside your browser. Names, dates and every other detail never leave your device.

How many certificates can bulk mode make?

Up to 200 certificates in one PDF per run. Paste one name per line; blank lines are ignored and repeated names are kept, so two students with the same name each get a certificate.

What size is the certificate?

Every certificate is a standard A4 page in landscape orientation, drawn at 150 dpi, so it prints sharply on any home or office printer. Choose 'Actual size' or 100% in your printer dialog for an exact fit.

Can I change the design?

Yes. There are three built-in designs - Elegant Gold, Modern Blue and Classic - and every word on the certificate is editable: the title, recipient name, course line, description, date, issuer and signature name.

How it works

The certificate is drawn on an HTML canvas at full A4 landscape size (297 x 210 mm at 150 dpi - 1754 x 1240 pixels). Every element is drawn fresh from your text each time you type: the double-rule border and corner flourishes, the title, the gold seal with its ribbon tails, and the signature line in a script style. When you download the PDF, the finished drawing is placed on an A4 landscape page by the pdf-lib library - the same library our other PDF tools use - so the file prints exactly as the preview looks. In bulk mode the same drawing is repeated once per name into a single multi-page PDF, with a progress bar so you can watch a long list finish.

Worked example

A teacher finishes a two-day training workshop with 28 participants. She opens Bulk mode, keeps the title "Certificate of Participation", sets the course line to her workshop name, pastes the 28 names from her register - one per line - and clicks Generate. A minute later she has one 28-page PDF; she prints it double-checked against the register, signs each page above the printed signature line, and hands the certificates out the same day. No design software, no upload of her students' names, and no fee.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add my own logo or a handwritten signature image?

Not in this version - the design uses the built-in seal and a printed signature name. Many people print the certificate and sign it by hand above the signature line, which looks more personal anyway.

Why does the name shrink on some certificates?

Very long names are automatically reduced in size so they always fit on one line inside the border. Shortening a middle name usually keeps the larger, grander size.

Does bulk mode change anything besides the name?

No. Title, course, description, date, issuer, signature and design are identical on every page - only the recipient name changes, exactly like a printed stack filled in one name at a time.
