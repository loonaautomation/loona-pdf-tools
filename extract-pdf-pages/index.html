Extract Pages from PDF Online - Free

In short: the Extract PDF Pages tool copies only the pages you choose - for example page 3 and pages 5 to 8 - into a brand-new PDF, in the original order.

When a 60-page report has just four pages you actually need, extract them. Type the pages, download a slim new PDF, and share exactly the right content - nothing more, nothing less.

Private by design: your files are processed 100% in your browser with JavaScript. They are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to extract pages from a PDF

  1. Choose the source PDF and check its page count.
  2. Type the pages to keep, for example 3, 5-8.
  3. Click "Extract Pages" and download the new PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What order do extracted pages follow?

Pages always come out in their original document order, no matter how you type the list.

Is the original file changed?

Never. Extraction builds a new file; your original stays untouched.

Can I extract a single page?

Yes - just type its number, like 7.

Does extraction reduce file quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly, with text and images intact.
