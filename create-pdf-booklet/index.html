Create PDF Booklet Online - Free

In short: the Create PDF Booklet tool imposes your PDF in real booklet order: pages are paired on landscape sheets (last page beside first, and so on) so that printing double-sided, folding the stack in half and stapling the spine gives a booklet that reads page 1, 2, 3... in order.

Booklet order looks wrong on screen - that is the point. When the printed stack is folded, every page lands in its reading place. This tool does the imposition maths for you, including padding with blanks to a multiple of four.

Printing instructions: print the output double-sided, flip on short edge, at Actual size (100%). Then fold the whole stack in half and staple along the fold. Test with one copy first - printers differ in how they flip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the page order look scrambled?

That is imposition. For an 8-page booklet the sheets carry (8|1), (2|7), (6|3), (4|5). Folded, page 1 faces page 2, and the booklet reads in order. The on-screen scramble is exactly what makes the folded result correct.

What if my page count is not a multiple of four?

A folded sheet holds four booklet pages, so documents are padded with blank pages at the end up to the next multiple of four - the tool tells you how many blanks were added. Those blanks land at the back of the booklet.

Which duplex setting is correct?

Short-edge flip (sometimes called 'flip on short edge' or 'top binding' for landscape). Long-edge flip would put alternate sheets upside down. Always test one copy before a big run.

Will my pages stay sharp?

Yes - pages are embedded and scaled as vector content, not images. Choosing 'Exact double size' avoids scaling entirely when your pages are exactly half the sheet (A5 pages on A4 landscape, for example).

How it works

The tool counts your pages and pads the sequence with blanks to a multiple of four. It then builds the classic saddle-stitch imposition sequence - for N pages: N, 1, 2, N-1, N-2, 3, 4, N-3 ... - and draws each consecutive pair side by side on a landscape sheet with pdf-lib, scaling each page to fit its half. Printed double-sided with a short-edge flip and folded, the sheets nest into a booklet in perfect reading order.

Worked example

An 11-page school magazine is padded to 12 pages (1 blank added). The booklet file has 6 sheet-sides; printed double-sided on A4 landscape, folded and stapled, it becomes a 12-page A5 magazine that reads from cover to back page in order.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a booklet from A4 pages?

Yes - choose A4 landscape sheets and each A4 page is scaled down to an A5 booklet page. Text shrinks accordingly, so start from reasonably large type.

Where do the added blanks end up?

Padding is appended after your last page before imposing, which places blanks at the end of the booklet (and possibly facing the last content page) - the natural place for them.

Does this staple or finish the booklet?

No - this prepares the print-ready file. Folding and stapling (ideally with a long-arm stapler) happen at the printer.
