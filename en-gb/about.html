About Loona PDF Tools

In short: Loona PDF Tools is a free set of 16 PDF tools by Loona Automation that runs entirely in your browser - your documents are never uploaded anywhere.

What is Loona PDF Tools?

Loona PDF Tools is a free collection of 16 online tools for everyday PDF work: merging, splitting, compressing, converting between PDF and images, extracting text, rotating, deleting and extracting pages, adding page numbers and watermarks, signing documents and editing PDF metadata.

It is part of Loona Automation, a family of free, problem-solving web tools built for students, teachers, freelancers, offices and small businesses - in Pakistan and around the world.

Our Difference: Privacy by Design

Almost every PDF website works by uploading your documents to a server. We built the opposite: all processing happens inside your own browser with JavaScript. Your files are never transmitted, never stored and never seen by us. It is faster, it works on any device, and it is simply the respectful way to handle people's documents.

Our Mission

Loona Automation's mission - "Bolti Duniya" - is to make useful technology speak everyone's language: free tools that solve real problems, without sign-ups, paywalls or fine print. If a tool saves you five minutes today, it has done its job.
