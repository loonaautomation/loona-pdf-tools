How to edit a PDF

  1. Click the box above and choose your PDF. The file opens right here in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
  2. Pick a tool: Text, Rectangle, Highlight, Whiteout or Draw — or add an image or a signature.
  3. Click or drag on the page to place the element. Use the Select tool to move it, drag the gold corner to resize it, or delete it if you change your mind.
  4. Move between pages with the Previous / Next buttons or the page dropdown. Every page keeps its own elements, so you can edit a long document page by page.
  5. When everything looks right, click "Export Edited PDF". Your additions are flattened onto the original pages and the finished file downloads as your-file-edited.pdf.

What this editor can and cannot do

This is an overlay editor, and we would rather tell you plainly what that means than let you discover it later.

What it can do

  • Add new text anywhere on a page, in five classic fonts (Helvetica, Helvetica Bold, Times, Times Bold and Courier), at any size and colour.
  • Add images — a logo, a stamp, a photo — and place a signature that you draw with mouse or finger, or type as your name.
  • Draw rectangles and highlights to mark up a document, and draw freehand with the pen tool.
  • Cover mistakes with whiteout: draw an opaque block over a wrong word or number, then type the correction on top of it.
  • Move, resize and delete anything you added, on any page, and export one finished, flattened PDF.

What it cannot do

  • It does not change, move or reflow the PDF's original text like a word processor. The words already in the file stay exactly where they are.
  • To fix a typo, cover the wrong word with whiteout and type the correction over it — that is the honest workaround, and it works well for forms, invoices and letters.
  • Whiteout here is a visual cover: the covered content is still inside the file underneath the block. If you must permanently remove sensitive content before sharing, use our Redact PDF tool instead, which burns the boxes into rebuilt page images.
  • It cannot open password-protected PDFs, and it is not a form filler — for existing form fields use Fill PDF Form.

Privacy — your file never leaves your device

Every step — opening, rendering, editing and exporting — runs 100% in your browser with JavaScript (PDF.js renders the pages, pdf-lib writes the finished file). Your PDF is never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us, and the editor itself makes no network requests while you work. Close the tab and everything is gone. There is no sign-up, no page limit and no watermark.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it change the original words in my PDF?

No. The Loona PDF Editor adds and covers content on top of the page; it cannot rewrite, move or reflow the text that is already in the file. To fix a wrong word, draw a whiteout block over it and type the correction on top — visually the page then reads exactly as you want it.

How do I fix a typo in a PDF with this editor?

Choose the Whiteout tool and drag a block over the wrong word. Then choose the Text tool (or the Add Text button), type the corrected word in a matching font and size, and drag it into place over the whiteout. Export, and the finished page shows only the correction.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The editor runs entirely in your browser: PDF.js draws the pages on screen and pdf-lib builds the finished file on your device. Nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by us, and there is no sign-up.

Is the Loona PDF Editor really free?

Yes — free with no sign-up, no page limits and no watermark on the exported file. You can edit as many PDFs as you like.

Can I sign a PDF in this editor?

Yes. Click "Add Signature", draw your signature on the pad with mouse or finger — or type your name to turn it into a signature image — and it is placed on the current page as a movable, resizable image. It is a visual signature, not a certificate-based digital signature; for a signature-only job you can also use our dedicated Sign PDF tool.

Does whiteout permanently remove what it covers?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. Whiteout draws an opaque cover over the content, so it no longer shows or prints — but the original content still exists underneath inside the file. If the content is sensitive and must be gone for good, use our Redact PDF tool, which rebuilds each page as an image with the boxes burned in.

Which fonts can I use for the text I add?

Five standard PDF fonts: Helvetica, Helvetica Bold, Times, Times Bold and Courier, in any size and colour. Because these fonts are built into every PDF reader, your added text displays correctly everywhere without making the file larger.

Will my elements stay in the right place?

Yes. Elements are stored per page in true PDF coordinates (points), not screen pixels, so when the file is exported each element is drawn onto its page at exactly the position, size and order you arranged — whatever the page size.

How it works

When you open a file, PDF.js renders the current page onto a canvas in your browser. Everything you add — a text box, an image, a rectangle, a highlight, a whiteout block, a freehand stroke or a signature — is drawn on a transparent layer above that canvas and stored for that page in true PDF coordinates, measured in points. Switching pages never mixes elements up: each page keeps its own list, in the order you added them. On export, pdf-lib opens your original file and draws every element onto its page at those same coordinates, converting the on-screen top-down positions into the PDF's own coordinate system, then saves the result with -edited in the name. Because the original pages are kept and only your additions are drawn on top, the output stays a real, selectable-text PDF — unlike tools that rebuild pages as pictures.

Worked example

An invoice PDF shows the wrong total, "Rs 4,500", and is missing your business logo. You drag a whiteout block over the old total, add the text "Rs 5,400" in Helvetica Bold over it, place your logo image at the top of the page, and sign the bottom by typing your name in the signature box. One click on Export Edited PDF downloads invoice-edited.pdf — corrected, branded and signed, with the rest of the invoice untouched.
